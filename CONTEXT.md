# SiteEN DAILY

SiteEN DAILY organizes construction documents and site photos for companies with multiple projects. A person signs in with one user account and receives access through company membership and project assignments.

## Language

**User account (บัญชีผู้ใช้)**: A person's sign-in identity. Having an account does not grant access to a company's projects.

**Company (บริษัท)**: An organization that owns its projects and documents. Each company's information is separate from other companies.
_Avoid_: Account when referring to a company.

**Company owner (Owner)**: A member who manages their company, its projects, invitations, and project assignments, and can access all of that company's projects. Ownership of one company grants no access to another company.
_Avoid_: Platform administrator when referring to a company owner.

**Company membership (สมาชิกบริษัท)**: A user's relationship with a company. Ordinary membership alone does not grant access to its projects under the proposed project access model.

**Project assignment (การมอบหมายโครงการ)**: An explicit grant to a company member for a particular project. One person may have several assignments, each with its own role.

**Project editor (Editor)**: An assigned member who can view and edit the project's work documents and site photos.
_Avoid_: Company editor for the proposed project access model.

**Project viewer (Viewer)**: An assigned member who can view the project's information and export its PDFs, without editing.

**Company invitation (คำเชิญเข้าบริษัท)**: An owner's offer to join a company with specified project assignments. Sending an invitation grants no company or project access; the recipient must verify the invited email and accept it.

**Daily Request**: A planned work request prepared before work and sent as a PDF for external signature.

**Daily Report**: A record of actual work, issues, resources, and site photos for a project and date.
